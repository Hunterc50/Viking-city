# Publishing Viking City so friends can play

You do this once. It takes about 10 minutes. The two pictures are in the
`publish` folder of this project: `icon.png` (512 x 512) and
`thumbnail.png` (1920 x 1080).

## 1. Publish the place

1. Open **VikingCity.rbxl** in Roblox Studio.
2. Click **File** (top left), then **Publish to Roblox**.
3. Choose **Create new experience**.
4. Name: `Viking City`
5. Description (copy and paste):

   > Raise a Viking village by the fjord. Chop, mine and hunt, build longhouses
   > and farms, and grow your clan from Settler to King. Build longships, sink
   > raiders, storm rival holds and sail to Skerholm for the richest plunder.
   > Defend your village from warbands, and raid your friends' towns for loot.

6. Genre: **Strategy** (or **Adventure**). Devices: tick Computer, Phone and Tablet.
7. Click **Create**. Wait until it says it is published.

## 2. Turn on saving

Without this, villages are not saved.

1. In Studio click **File**, then **Game Settings**.
2. Click **Security** on the left.
3. Switch on **Enable Studio Access to API Services**.
4. Click **Save**.

## 3. Set the server size to 8

The valley has 8 village plots, so a server must not hold more than 8 people.

1. Go to **create.roblox.com** and sign in.
2. Click **Creations**, then click **Viking City**.
3. On the left click **Places**, then click the place, then **Access** (or **Configure Place**).
4. Set **Server Fill / Max Players** to **8**. Click **Save**.

## 4. Add the icon and thumbnail

Still on create.roblox.com, in **Viking City**:

1. On the left click **Basic Info** (or **Configure > Basic Info**).
2. Under **Icon**, click the picture and upload `publish/icon.png`.
3. Under **Thumbnails**, click **Add** and upload `publish/thumbnail.png`.
4. Optional but good: add 2 or 3 of your own screenshots from the game. In
   Studio press Play, set up a nice view (your village, a longship, a raid) and
   press the camera button in the Roblox menu (Esc) or use the
   **Print Screen** key. Roblox likes 1920 x 1080 pictures.
5. Click **Save**.

## 5. Fill in the maturity questions

Roblox will not let an experience go public before this.

1. On the left click **Questionnaire** (under Audience / Maturity).
2. Answer honestly. For this game: mild fantasy violence (axes, swords, bows,
   no blood), no romance, no gambling, no real-money items. Submit.

## 6. Let friends in

1. On the left click **Overview** (or **Audience**).
2. Choose who can play:
   - **Friends only**: pick **Private**, then in Studio go to
     **File > Game Settings > Permissions** and add your friends by name
     (set them to **Play**). Or make a private server: see step 7.
   - **Everyone**: switch the experience to **Public**.
3. Click **Save**.

## 7. (Optional) A private server for you and your friends

1. On create.roblox.com, in **Viking City**, open **Monetization > Private Servers**.
2. Switch on **Allow Private Servers** and set the price to **Free**. Save.
3. Open the game page on roblox.com, click **Servers**, then
   **Create Private Server**, and invite your friends.

## Updating the game later

Open the newest VikingCity.rbxl, click **File > Publish to Roblox**, and pick
the **Viking City** experience you already made. Players get the new version
the next time they join (or after you click **Shut down all servers** on the
game page).
